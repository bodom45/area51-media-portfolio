The Story

Brandon Odom — Founder & Creative Director

Director. Producer. Editor. I'm a Dallas–Fort Worth media entrepreneur who built Area 51 Radio from the ground up — shooting, cutting and marketing the content myself before scaling a team around it.

Area 51 Radio is the new-age MTV: a platform and production house that gives independent artists the visuals, the stage and the marketing muscle to profit from their creativity — without waiting on a major label. From cinematic music videos and live cyphers to short-form that goes viral, the work is built to travel and to convert.
